\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\maybeincludegraphics[width=0.98\textwidth]{artifacts/arbitrage_paths_crossed_noncrossed.pdf}
\caption{Real quoted versus parity-implied (synthetic) option market. When the markets are crossed and disjoint ($C_{ask}<C^{\mathrm{syn}}_{bid}$, or the symmetric case), there is a single guaranteed riskless arbitrage. When one market is nested inside the other, or when the two straddle each other, there is no guaranteed profit but two paths carry a \emph{potential} market-maker advantage, realized only if the maker's quote is joined and then hit. In the nested case these are the forward and backward join-call paths ($C^{\mathrm{syn}}_{bid}-C_{bid}$ and $C_{ask}-C^{\mathrm{syn}}_{ask}$), with the join-put payoffs equal to their negatives; in the straddling case they are the forward join-call and forward join-put paths ($C^{\mathrm{syn}}_{bid}-C_{bid}$ and $C^{\mathrm{syn}}_{ask}-C_{ask}$). The latter two configurations are what AMU aggregates. Schematic, illustrative levels.}
\label{fig:arb_paths_crossed_noncrossed}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[htbp]

The band centres are the frictionless (no-cost) synthetic prices $P_{bid}+(F_{bid}-K)e^{-rT}$ and $P_{ask}+(F_{ask}-K)e^{-rT}$; the trading cost buys the $\pm\mathrm{cost}$ cushion around each. As costs shrink, the real market is squeezed onto the synthetic market. Schematic, illustrative levels.}

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Data-processing assumptions for MMA/AMU construction.}
\label{tab:data_processing_assumptions}
\begin{tabularx}{0.95\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Component & Assumption \\
\midrule
Trading-cost parameter & \texttt{cost\_per\_notional} = \CostPerNotional. The cost term is \texttt{cost\_per\_notional} multiplied by index level. \\
AMU clipping rule & MMA series are clipped to $[0,\MaxAmuBps]$ basis points before positive-part averaging. \\
MMA clipping rule & MMA series are clipped to $[-\MaxMmaBps,+\MaxMmaBps]$ basis points. \\
Spread filter & Call and put spreads are capped at $\SpreadBpMax$ basis points. \\
Relative-strike filter & Allowed range is $[\RelStrikeMin,\RelStrikeMax]$. \\
Minimum quote size & \texttt{min\_quote\_size\_dollar} must be at least \MinQuoteSizeDollar. \\
Subsampling & Last-known observation carried forward to the configured interval (default \texttt{\SampleFreqDefault}); a leg with a missing or non-updated bid/ask price or size is marked stale.\\
Stale-quote filter & \FilterStale{}: when enabled, ticks with a stale call, put, or spot leg are dropped before any AMU/MMA aggregation. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\begin{table}[htbp]

{\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}\normalfont\normalsize\input{artifacts/amu_summary_table.tex}}
\caption{Global AMU summary across exchanges and underlyings. AMU is the positive-part mean of MMA, in basis points of notional: \emph{cond} averages over the executable (positive-MMA) paths only, \emph{uncond} over all paths (equivalently, \emph{cond} times the fraction of paths that are executable). A \emph{path} is one of the four put--call-parity join paths (forward/backward $\times$ join-call/join-put) evaluated at a single synchronized quote; each matched put--call pair contributes four paths. The last two columns count ticks: \emph{positive MMA} is the number of synchronized ticks with at least one executable path, and \emph{num pairs} the number of matched put--call pair ticks.}
\label{tab:amu_summary}
\end{table}
