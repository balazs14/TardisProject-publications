\begin{frame}{Table 3 --- Data-processing assumptions}
  \vfill\centering
  \colorbox{white}{\color{black}\adjustbox{width=\linewidth,totalheight=0.82\textheight,keepaspectratio}{\footnotesize
  \begin{tabular}{@{}l p{8.6cm}@{}}
  \toprule
  Component & Assumption \\
  \midrule
  AMU clipping rule & MMA series clipped to $[0,\MaxAmuBps]$ bp before positive-part averaging. \\
  MMA clipping rule & MMA series clipped to $[-\MaxMmaBps,+\MaxMmaBps]$ bp. \\
  Spread filter & Call and put spreads capped at $\SpreadBpMax$ bp. \\
  Relative-strike filter & Allowed range $[\RelStrikeMin,\RelStrikeMax]$. \\
  Minimum quote size & \texttt{min\_quote\_size\_dollar} at least \MinQuoteSizeDollar. \\
  Subsampling & Last observation carried forward to the interval (default \texttt{\SampleFreqDefault}); a missing/non-updated leg is marked stale. \\
  Stale-quote filter & \FilterStale{}: when on, ticks with a stale leg are dropped before aggregation. \\
  Discount rate & $r=5\%$, entering only through $e^{-rT}$. Not crucial (see sensitivity analysis). \\
  Trading cost & \CostBp~bp of notional (\texttt{cost\_per\_notional}$=\CostPerNotional$). Not crucial (see sensitivity analysis). \\
  \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}}}
  \vfill
\end{frame}
